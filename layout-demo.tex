\section*{Room for the reader}
The same page and seed are used for every version of this example. Only the layout
controls change. Full density keeps the original garden. Balanced and airy settings
space the leaves farther apart, limit secondary branches and reduce small details.

The large flowers and the main stems still give the garden its structure. Density is
an amount of ornament, not a percentage of ink or a change in opacity; the exact result
depends on the seed, the available region and the season.

Whitespace has its own controls. The space below this paragraph can stay empty,
the footer and header decorations can be omitted, and the gap beside the text can be
made wider. None of these controls moves or reflows the text.
